# PDF Tools for iOS and iPadOS: Privacy Policy

PDF Tools processes your documents on your device. The app does not upload your
documents, images, passwords, or signatures to the developer, and it does not
include advertising or analytics tracking.

When you choose a document or image, the app makes a working copy in its
temporary storage. It also stores the results there until you save or share
them. The app attempts to remove its temporary workspace the next time it
starts; iOS may also remove temporary files. Save any result you want to keep
using the system share or save controls.

Files you open or save using a cloud storage provider, and files you share
using another app, are subject to that service's privacy practices. Links in
PDF Tools open third-party websites, including GitHub and Buy Me a Coffee,
whose privacy practices also apply when you visit them.

For questions or support, contact the developer through the
[PDF Tools issue tracker](https://github.com/ivadim/pdf-tools/issues).
