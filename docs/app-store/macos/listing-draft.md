# macOS App Store listing draft — English (U.S.)

Draft for review. The promotional text, description, and review notes below
were saved to the macOS 1.0 draft in App Store Connect on 14 November 2026.

## Promotional text

Merge, compress, redact and more. Work with PDFs on your Mac, with processing on your device.

## Description

Get everyday PDF jobs done on your Mac. PDF Tools processes documents on your device, with no account, ads or tracking.

Bring pages together, split a document, or rearrange and rotate pages. Preview your work, choose your options, then save the result.

• Merge PDFs and put pages in order.
• Split documents and extract selected pages.
• Compress PDFs with a choice of settings.
• Protect a PDF with a password or unlock a document you can access.
• Add page numbers, watermarks or a signature, and edit document details.
• Redact marked areas in a separate copy, convert pages to grayscale, or make a PDF from images.

Redacted copies are image-only: text is no longer selectable, and links, forms and document details are removed.

Choose files on your Mac, preview the result, and save a separate copy.

## Review notes draft

No sign-in is required. Choose a PDF or image using the file picker, or drag one into the window. Select a tool, review its options and preview, then save the result. Processing happens on the Mac; documents are not uploaded to a server.
