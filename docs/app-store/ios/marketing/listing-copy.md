# Proposed App Store copy — English

## Name

PDF Tools: Free & Private

## Subtitle

PDF editing on your device

## Promotional text

Merge, compress, redact and more. Work with PDFs on your iPhone or iPad, with processing on your device.

## Description

Get everyday PDF jobs done on your iPhone or iPad. PDF Tools processes documents on your device, with no account, ads or tracking.

Bring pages together, split a document, or rearrange and rotate pages. Preview your work, choose your options, then save or share the result.

• Merge PDFs and put pages in order.
• Split documents and extract selected pages.
• Compress PDFs with a choice of settings.
• Protect a PDF with a password or unlock a document you can access.
• Add page numbers, watermarks or a signature, and edit document details.
• Redact marked areas in a separate copy, convert pages to grayscale, or make a PDF from images.

Redacted copies are image-only: text is no longer selectable, and links, forms and document details are removed.

Choose files from your device or a file provider and work with them locally. Your original document remains available separately.
