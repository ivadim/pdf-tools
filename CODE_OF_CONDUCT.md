# Code of conduct

PDF Tools follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful in issues, pull requests and anywhere else the project is discussed. Harassment, personal attacks, and sexualised or discriminatory language or imagery aren't acceptable. Neither is publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, tell the maintainer, [@ivadim](https://github.com/ivadim), privately: the [private reporting form](https://github.com/ivadim/pdf-tools/security/advisories/new) works for this too (start the title with "Code of conduct"). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
